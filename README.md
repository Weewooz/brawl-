# Capture Point

Mobile-first isometric Katana and Yumi combat, built on the Yumi framework, Blink, and Rojo. The persistent arena RPG roadmap is in [tasks/todo.md](tasks/todo.md).

## Finding the code

- `src/Client/Controllers/Systems`: input, camera, combat presentation, Capture HUD, and Dungeon UI. A system's `init.lua` is its entry point; child modules handle its supporting responsibilities.
- `src/Server/Systems`: authoritative combat, matches, dungeons, and profiles.
- `src/Shared/Combat` and `src/Shared/Capture`: weapon definitions, configuration, and rules used by both sides.
- `src/Shared/Core`: framework registration in `Framework/Features.lua`, profile shape in `Profile/Template.lua`, and tags.
- `src/Shared/Utilities`: reusable helpers. `src/Client/Modules` contains client presentation helpers.
- `src/Assets`: authored Roblox instances and artwork; combat audio/highlight pools are in `Assets/Combat`.
- `network.blink`: network contracts. `src/Shared/Network` is generated by Blink.
- `default.project.json`: maps source folders and assets into Roblox services.

Start with the relevant client or server system, then follow its requires. Client startup is `src/Client/Preload/Client/init.client.lua`; server startup is `src/Server/Server.server.lua`.

Inside server `Combat`, `Attacks` validates melee requests, `Hits` resolves damage and guards, and `Characters` handles profiles and equipment. Inside client `Combat`, `Feedback` handles hit audio/highlights, `CharacterAnimations` owns tracks, and `InputActionController` owns native action bindings and touch gestures. Each `Combat/init.lua` coordinates those modules and keeps the public combat API.

`InputActionController` creates client-owned `InputContext`/`InputAction`/`InputBinding` instances for desktop actions and Sprint. Spin and mouse Dash use successful GUI clicks; aimed touch controls keep pointer tracking for drag/release cancellation. Movement locks stay in the existing `Input` system.

Keep implementation helpers beneath their owning module. A tested module uses `Name/init.lua` with `Name/Name.spec.lua`, which becomes a child ModuleScript in Studio; callers still require `Name`. Add bootable systems to `Features.lua`; helpers and specs do not need registration. The server validates requests and owns saved data and match outcomes.

`Packages`, `ServerPackages`, `sourcemap.json`, and `src/Shared/Network` are generated. `tmp`, `work`, and `outputs` contain authoring tools, intermediate exports, and deliverables; they are outside the runtime source tree.

## Match rules

- Up to five players per team. A match starts once both teams have a player.
- Capture the central point in three seconds. Neutralize enemy control before capturing it.
- Control earns one point per second; enemy occupation pauses scoring. Both teams inside contest the point.
- First to 100 wins. Three-minute rounds can enter up to one minute of overtime.
- Five-second respawns, two-second spawn protection, automatic rematches.

Mobile: left stick moves; hold Slash to keep attacking, drag Slash or Guard to aim, and drag/release Dash. Hold Rising Crash or Ground Shock for an aim preview, then release to cast; drag into the cancel region to cancel. Charge previews its locked target. Wind Spin casts on tap. Desktop: hold LMB to attack, F Guard, E Dash; 1 Rising Crash, 2 Wind Spin, 3 Charge, 4 Ground Shock. Options reduces shake or flashes.

Skills buffer during the final 0.15 seconds of an action or cooldown. A buffered skill takes priority over held Slash; death, guard, or interruption clears it.

### Sword fighter

- **Charge:** teleports toward the selected enemy within 30 studs; 5 damage, 40% slow for 0.4 seconds, 6-second cooldown, 20 stamina. Unsafe or obstructed destinations reject the cast.
- **Rising Crash:** narrow frontal cut within 5 studs after a 0.22-second windup; 18 damage and Exposed for 3 seconds, increasing received damage by 10% without stacking. 7-second cooldown, 20 stamina.
- **Wind Spin:** two 8-damage sweeps at 0.2 and 0.5 seconds, within 6 studs; reduced movement during its 0.7-second duration. 8-second cooldown, 25 stamina.
- **Ground Shock:** frontal wave within 7 studs after a 0.25-second windup; 8 damage, a collision-checked push up to 3 studs, and 25% slow for 1 second. 10-second cooldown, 20 stamina.
- Guard, evasive Dash, cover, and interrupting the windup provide counterplay. Slows expire independently; the strongest active slow applies.
- **Perfect Guard:** the first 0.18 seconds of a frontal guard can parry one strike for 3 stamina. It interrupts the attacker for 0.2 seconds while allowing movement. Reopening the window requires 0.6 seconds; holding guard continues normal blocking.

### Yumi archer

Select Katana or Yumi in the lobby or training area. Selection is saved on the character profile and restored on respawn; queue, match, and dungeon activity lock selection.

- Hold LMB or the mobile Draw button, then release: 10–28 damage over one second of server-timed charge, 60-stud range, 8 stamina, and a 0.8-second cooldown. Drawing slows movement.
- **1 / Piercing Shot:** 24 damage to up to two opponents, 60-stud range, 20 stamina, 7-second cooldown. Cover stops the shot.
- **2 / Volley:** three arrows in a spread, 8 damage each, 45-stud range, 25 stamina, 8-second cooldown.
- **3 / Quick Shot:** 10 damage, 45-stud range, 12 stamina, 4-second cooldown.
- **4 / Pinning Shot:** 12 damage and 35% slow for two seconds on a damaging hit, 50-stud range, 20 stamina, 10-second cooldown.

Guard, Dash, incapacitation, weapon changes, and activity changes cancel draws. PC mouse aiming raycasts the cursor onto world surfaces, ignoring combatants and visual effects. The preview uses the hit surface's height and updates after the camera; portrait locking can override mouse aim. Touch drag aiming retains its existing controls.

### Team feedback

The teammate strip shows health and respawn time. Attack and Defend ping the capture point; Regroup pings your position. Pings reach teammates in the same round, last three seconds, and have a 1.5-second cooldown. The death recap shows up to four recent damage events with their source, skill, and effects.

The Crest Courtyard arena has protected spawn approaches, flank lanes, and cover around the capture point. Nearby remote skill windups use pooled spatial audio.

### Portrait soft lock

- Tap a nearby enemy portrait to select it, tap again to release, or tap another to switch.
- Slash, Rising Crash, and Ground Shock aim toward the selected enemy; Slash drag aiming overrides the lock for that gesture. Charge requires a selected enemy and teleports to melee distance facing them. Movement, Guard, Wind Spin, and evasive Dash keep their existing controls.
- Acquisition range is 30 studs; a selected target remains eligible to 34 studs. Cover, leaving the screen, death, protection, team changes, or leaving the match release it. Locking does not extend attack range or automatically chase.
- Five authored portrait slots and one marker are reused. Player headshots are cached; NPCs use a `Portrait` image attribute, their face decal, or the authored face fallback. No targeting instances are created during play.
- Based on Riot's [Wild Rift portrait-lock controls](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/pl-pl/news/game-updates/sterowanie-w-wild-rift/).

```powershell
rokit install
wally install
blink network.blink
rojo sourcemap default.project.json -o sourcemap.json
wally-package-types --sourcemap sourcemap.json Packages/

rojo serve
```

## Studio testing

Connect Rojo and press Play: Studio starts in the training area with combat and portrait locking enabled, without queuing. Use the compact **Play Capture** button to queue; leaving the queue returns to practice. Respawning also returns to practice when outside a match. Published servers retain the normal lobby.

Solo **Play Capture** starts 5v5 practice by default: one human and four allied bots against five enemy bots. Bots capture, contest, guard, use skills, and respawn after five seconds using the same server-owned rigs and weapons. **Leave Practice** returns to training. Set `Workspace.CaptureArena.BotsPerTeam` to 2 for lighter 3v3 practice, or disable `PracticeBotsEnabled` to test human-only matchmaking. Bots are restricted to Studio; published matches use humans.

Pure rule and combat-authority checks run in the server Command Bar:

```lua
local capture = game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.Capture
print(require(capture.Rules["Rules.spec"])(require(capture.Rules)))
print(require(capture.Authority["Authority.spec"])(require(capture.Authority)))
local combat = game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.Combat
print(require(combat.Weapons["Weapons.spec"])(require(combat.Weapons)))
print(require(combat.YumiRules["YumiRules.spec"])(require(combat.YumiRules)))
```

Input rules and PC projection checks run in the client Command Bar:

```lua
local combat = game.ReplicatedStorage.Client.Controllers.Systems.Combat
require(combat.InputRules["InputRules.spec"])(require(combat.InputRules))
print("Input rules passed")
print(require(combat.InputActionController["InputActionController.spec"])(require(combat.InputActionController)))
print(require(combat.MouseAim["MouseAim.spec"])(require(combat.MouseAim), workspace.CurrentCamera))
```

UI, arena geometry, animation sources, markers, telegraphs, and audio were authored in Studio and exported to Rojo project files. AudioPlayers, highlights, markers, and telegraphs use reusable pools. Character spawning and per-character physics remain engine/runtime work.

Eight original engraved crest icons were generated with the built-in image generator and uploaded through Studio MCP. PNGs, prompts, and Roblox asset IDs are in `src/Assets/Artwork`.

## Notes

- Rules: `src/Shared/Capture/Rules/init.lua`; server authority: `src/Server/Systems/Capture`.
- Capture and Dungeon are enabled in `Features.lua`. Start a dungeon from its town portal or with `/dungeon Easy`, `/dungeon Normal`, or `/dungeon Hard`.
- [Brawl Stars Hot Zone](https://supercell.com/en/games/brawlstars/blog/game-updates/update-taras-bazaar/) informed the objective focus. Our contested point deliberately pauses scoring for both teams.
- [Roblox mobile layout guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/building-and-visuals/ui/positioning-and-sizing-guiobjects) informed thumb controls and safe-area layouts.
- Ten-client load, latency, and team balance still require multiplayer playtesting.
